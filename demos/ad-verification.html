      How the match works and what the proof establishes

      The receiving app establishes delivery. The receipt identifies the placement and creative. This demo requires the app to sign both received and viewable flags and report at least 50% of the ad visible for one continuous second. Display is measurable. A person’s attention is outside that measurement.

      Regional partners observe visits. The simulated app detects a signed destination beacon and presents a previously authorized campaign credential to the local gateway. The gateway signs that encounter. Visitors can continue their trip without checking in. Coverage extends only to participating stops.

      Matching requires permission. A keyed hash ties the private matching token to this campaign. The independent verifier checks each source signature and looks for an eligible regional stop after a signed view, within 30 days. One device can visit several stops and still counts once.

      The public proof checks the signed account. The export contains aggregate batches and evidence fingerprints. An authorized auditor needs the private receipts to repeat the count. A signer could still make a false observation, and a later visit alone cannot establish that the ad caused the trip.

      All participants run in this browser. The observations and locations are fictional, and each new session generates its own signing keys. Reloading or resetting starts a new session.

      Why share a record?

      Campaign results can affect decisions and payment. If a visit is later removed as a duplicate, everyone can see the original report, who submitted it, and the correction.

      1. 1. Watch reports arrivePartners report ads and visits.
      2. 2. Challenge a totalA changed old report fails the check.
      3. 3. Add a correctionThe earlier report stays visible.

      This checks what providers reported and whether example evidence fits the rule. It cannot prove a visit happened or that an ad caused it. All partner copies here are simulated in one browser.

        What this shared record can and cannot do

        Keep your existing measurement tools. This proposed audit layer preserves who submitted each result, the attribution rule used, fingerprints of supporting evidence, and later corrections. It does not replace measurement, provide identity matching, or establish causation.

        Private evidence, shared receipt. Raw example records live in a separate in-memory provider store. The ledger contains signed summaries, opaque receipt references and salted evidence fingerprints—not traveler IDs, page paths or location histories. The traveler labels in this interface are teaching annotations, not fields in the shared ledger. Even relationship metadata can be sensitive; a real deployment still needs access, consent and retention controls.

        Two different checks. Record verification checks signatures, block links and the original checkpoint. An evidence check also retrieves the provider material and reproduces the example 30-day attribution calculation. Unavailable evidence does not invalidate a signature, but prevents that independent calculation. Neither check proves an observation was accurate, complete, or caused by an advertisement.

        A local blockchain, not an independent network. SHA-256, ECDSA signatures, batched Merkle roots and three separately checked ledger copies operate here. All participants, keys and evidence are controlled by this browser session. This is not distributed consensus or real organizational independence. The ledger cannot prove that every relevant measurement was submitted.

        Compare the alternatives. A signed append-only audit log may be enough if all partners trust one operator. A jointly maintained blockchain needs a reason for shared governance, participating providers, authorized matching arrangements, key management and a dispute process. This demo is not a production architecture or a promise of integration.

        All people, locations, and dates are fictional examples. No real tourism destination is represented. Nothing connects to advertising, device, analytics or location services. Example days differ from accelerated playback time. Resetting or reloading discards the session. Admissions stop before 192 receipts so current travelers can finish; extra capacity is reserved for review and correction exercises.
