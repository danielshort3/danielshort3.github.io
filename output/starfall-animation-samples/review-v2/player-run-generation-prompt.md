# Player run study

Built-in image generation; review-only actor sheet. Raw generated PNG retained unchanged. Appearance references are not production replacements.

Use case: stylized-concept / identity-preserve.
Generate ONE transparent 8-pose RUN-CYCLE study for Project Starfall's compact human adventurer, for an actual frame-by-frame animation review.
Reference roles: image 1 is the existing adventurer's identity and proportions; image 2 is the current active player sheet (identity, costume and running action, not pose quality); image 3 is the approved Oracle clean finish reference only, not anatomy or colors.
Keep the original young compact adventurer recognizable: same large head to short-body ratio, tousled brown spiky hair silhouette and swept fringe, light warm skin, dark simple eyes looking right, off-white short-sleeved collared shirt with dark brown opening/trim, brown belt with one small square gold buckle, dark short trousers, brown leather wrist cuffs and brown boots with cuffs. No weapon, cape, helmet, backpack, new ornament or facial hair. Same hair spikes, outfit seams and body proportions throughout.
Style: clean illustrated 2D side-view RPG sprite, crisp dark contours, controlled shading, clear features, few readable material highlights, little noise. Match the approved Oracle's clarity, not its plant identity. No rigid pixel-grid, limited palette-count or prescribed hard-shading requirement. No fuzzy painting, shiny plastic or vector sticker finish.
LAYOUT: exactly FOUR columns by TWO rows, eight equally sized cells, wide 2:1 canvas, ideally 1536x768. Genuine transparent alpha background, invisible grid. Full character in every cell, generous padding; actor height approximately 58% of each cell, ground contact line at 82% cell height, pelvis centered at 50% cell width. No crossing rows, no cropped boots/hair. Same scale and camera throughout, facing RIGHT throughout. Neutral shoulder-to-hip and head dimensions invariant.
Action: one authentic bilateral running cycle IN PLACE. Draw eight distinct chronological articulated poses; far-side limbs are darker to identify their continuous motion. The near leg MUST alternate from front to back across the cycle. Do not repeat the same leading leg. Strong elbow and knee joint changes, solid human weight, a small natural body bob, subtle hair follow-through; no rubber body deformation.
Pose 0: near boot leading far forward/right, far boot trailing back/left, opposite arm swing; near heel about to contact.
Pose 1: near foot planted and body slightly lowered over it, far heel lifts and knee bends; arms pass toward middle.
Pose 2: near foot moves behind body with weight, far knee passes forward under torso, body rises.
Pose 3: brief flight/push-off: near boot extended back/left, far thigh drives forward/right with bent knee, opposite arm swing.
Pose 4: opposite contact, FAR boot leading forward/right and NEAR boot trailing backward/left; clearly different bilateral silhouette and limb overlap from pose 0.
Pose 5: far foot planted, body lowers slightly, near heel curls upward behind; arms pass toward middle opposite pose 1.
Pose 6: far foot supports behind torso, near knee passes forward under body; body rises opposite pose 2.
Pose 7: flight toward next near-foot contact: far boot extends back, near thigh forward with knee opening; naturally connects to pose 0, do not duplicate it.
Review timing: 600ms looping cycle, each pose75ms. Pure locomotion, no damage/contact event or effect category. Render only actor poses, no motion streaks, ground shadows, aura, dust, text, labels, numbers, panels or checkerboard. Do not simulate run by shifting or stretching a single drawing. Make a usable leg-alternating cycle. Keep all eight sprites complete and separate.
