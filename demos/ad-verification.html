    How this could fit your campaign

    Keep your existing measurement tools. This proposed audit layer preserves who submitted each result, the attribution rule used, fingerprints of supporting evidence, and later corrections. It does not replace measurement, provide identity matching, or establish causation.

    Private evidence, shared receipt. Raw example records live in a separate in-memory provider store. The ledger contains signed summaries, opaque receipt references and salted evidence fingerprints—not traveler IDs, page paths or location histories. The traveler labels in this interface are teaching annotations, not fields in the shared ledger. Even relationship metadata can be sensitive; a real deployment still needs access, consent and retention controls.

    Two different checks. Record verification checks signatures, block links and the original checkpoint. An evidence check also retrieves the provider material and reproduces the example 30-day attribution calculation. Unavailable evidence does not invalidate a signature, but prevents that independent calculation. Neither check proves an observation was accurate, complete, or caused by an advertisement.

    A local blockchain, not an independent network. SHA-256, ECDSA signatures, batched Merkle roots and three separately checked ledger copies operate here. All participants, keys and evidence are controlled by this browser session. This is not distributed consensus or real organizational independence. The ledger cannot prove that every relevant measurement was submitted.

    Compare the alternatives. A signed append-only audit log may be enough if all partners trust one operator. A jointly maintained blockchain needs a reason for shared governance, participating providers, authorized matching arrangements, key management and a dispute process. This demo is not a production architecture or a promise of integration.

    All people and dates are fictional. Nothing connects to advertising, device, analytics or location services. Example days differ from accelerated playback time. Resetting or reloading discards the session. Admissions stop before 192 receipts so current travelers can finish; extra capacity is reserved for review and correction exercises.
