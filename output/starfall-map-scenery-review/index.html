Starfall scenery review

Contextual artwork corrections. These compare source assets; the game review verifies their placement with actors, collision surfaces and effects.

Ashglass Pass: volcanic glass and basalt

Previously the same sandstone, grass and turquoise ore kit used by Quarry. The replacement uses dark volcanic glass, clean ash-gray contact edges and restrained amber mineral accents.

Starfall Crossing: architecture with a clear purpose

The original shared atlas remains available to other towns. Crossing now has its own fractured observatory, lens workshop, expedition depot, open frontier gate and distinct service kiosks.

Cinder Hollow: separate scenery from playable terrain

The volcano and warm ash atmosphere remain. Large foreground shelves and the flat road have become distant irregular rock silhouettes and ash haze, so the background stops advertising false footholds.

Shop interiors: four readable room identities

All 24 regional shops now share an illustrated room by vendor type. The previous rooms had no panorama and fell back to procedural outdoor scenery with houses and meadow grass. Vendor, exit and floor positions are preserved.
