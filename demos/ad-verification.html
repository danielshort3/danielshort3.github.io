Behind the demo

A mini blockchain, not a cryptocurrency. Four blocks contain real SHA-256 fingerprints and ECDSA P-256 signatures. Each block links to its predecessor; three local demo validators sign its hash. The original block count and last hash are saved separately as a checkpoint.

Record integrity is not proof of ad delivery. A valid signature shows that a record matches a signing key. It does not prove a human saw an ad, that a reported number is truthful, or that a visit occurred. Timestamps are fictional, not independently certified.

All participants run in this browser. These are simulated identities, not independent organizations or distributed consensus. No wallet, token, live advertising connection, or visitor tracking is used by the demo. Reloading starts a new local chain.

Export contains example records, public keys, and the session checkpoint—never private keys. It is self-contained demo evidence, not a third-party certificate.
